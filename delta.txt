ThreatPresence =
Meaning: Likelihood that an actual threat source exists in the scene (hostile actor, active hazard, weapon, intrusion) that could cause harm soon.
Strong cues: visible weapon/hazard, active assault, forced entry, aggressive pursuit, dangerous tool use near people, alarmed crowd reaction.
UNK rule: if the potential threat source is not visible/identifiable due to distance, occlusion, blur, or camera angle.
HostileIntentIndicators =
Meaning: Likelihood that a person/group intends to harm, intimidate, seize, sabotage, or attack (not just move quickly).
Strong cues: stalking/tailing, charging, ambush posture, cornering/blocking, targeting gestures, repeated advances after resistance, coordinated actor + spotter.
UNK rule: if body language, targets, or interaction context cannot be observed clearly.
WeaponsOrWeaponLikeObjects =
Meaning: Likelihood that a weapon or weapon-like object is present and usable (including improvised weapons).
Strong cues: gun/knife/club-like silhouettes, grip consistent with weapon use, drawing from waistband/bag, concealment + reveal, aiming posture, others recoiling.
UNK rule: if the object is partially occluded or too small/blurred to distinguish from benign items.
UnusualClusteringOrFormation =
Meaning: Likelihood that grouping/spacing is coordinated or abnormal for the setting, suggesting control, encirclement, blocking, or staging.
Strong cues: flanking/encirclement, line/wedge formation, synchronized movement, blocking exits/lanes, clustering around a target, unusually static “stand-and-watch” groups.
UNK rule: if crowd density is too high to resolve individuals or track relative positioning.
RouteDisturbanceOrRoadAnomaly =
Meaning: Likelihood that normal flow along paths/lanes is disrupted by an event, obstacle, or abnormal movement pattern.
Strong cues: wrong-way travel, abrupt congestion, sudden stop-and-go ripple, detours/avoidance, people/vehicles funneling around a new obstacle, panic dispersal.
UNK rule: if the route/lanes are not visible or baseline flow cannot be inferred from the frames.
PerimeterProbeOrBoundaryTest =
Meaning: Likelihood someone is testing a boundary/restricted zone to find access (physical or procedural) without committing fully.
Strong cues: repeated approach-and-withdraw, checking locks/handles, peeking around barriers, climbing/leaning over lines, timing guards/cameras, touching fence/gate seams.
UNK rule: if boundaries/signage/access points are not visible or the subject is too far/occluded.
TargetFixationOrSurveillanceBehavior =
Meaning: Likelihood an actor is surveilling or fixating on a specific target, entry, asset, or person (pre-attack, theft, or stalking behavior).
Strong cues: persistent staring/tracking, repeated look-backs, shadowing path, filming/zooming, pausing to watch, positioning for sightlines, timing target movements.
UNK rule: if gaze direction, camera/phone usage, or line-of-sight cannot be determined.
C2OrCommsIndicators (visual-only) =
Meaning: Likelihood of coordinated communication/command-and-control visible through gestures or device behavior (not audio).
Strong cues: hand signals, repeated phone checks timed with actions, earpiece/mic use, “spotter + actor” role split, relay behavior, synchronized start/stop.
UNK rule: if hands/devices are not visible or too small/blurred to confirm.
LogisticsFlowAnomaly =
Meaning: Likelihood that movement/transfer of items or luggage is out-of-pattern and potentially illicit (swap, drop, theft, unauthorized pickup).
Strong cues: hand-to-hand small item transfer (keys/cards/cash/badge), bag swap, quick concealment after transfer, unattended drop-off, pickup from hidden spot, transfer + immediate separation.
UNK rule: if objects/hands are occluded or item identity/transfer path is not visible.
EngineeringOrObstacleActivity =
Meaning: Likelihood someone is altering infrastructure or placing/removing obstacles to enable intrusion, delay response, or create hazards.
Strong cues: tampering with panels/doors/locks, placing barriers, blocking cameras/lines of sight, moving cones/bollards, disabling equipment, staging materials in lanes.
UNK rule: if the manipulated object/infrastructure is not visible or intent cannot be distinguished from normal maintenance.
IndirectFirePrepIndicators (visual-only) =
Meaning: Likelihood of visible preparation for indirect fire (mortar/rocket/artillery) based on equipment posture and setup behaviors.
Strong cues: tube/launcher-like equipment setup, loading motions, aiming elevation adjustments, concealment near firing position, spotter observing sky/impact area, rapid repositioning after setup.
UNK rule: if scene context is not militarized or relevant equipment is not visually discernible.
